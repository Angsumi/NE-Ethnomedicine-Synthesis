## Scientific checks before sending

The package formats the updated analysis; it is not an independent validation of the primary papers. The submission copy explicitly acknowledges the absent reproducible search log and the lack of a stratified source audit beyond the reported 100-unit pilot. If the authors have completed either, replace those limitations with the actual methods and findings. Do not describe those procedures as completed without records.

Confirm the existing pilot audit, source corrections, prespecified-rule claims, botanical identities, and source citations against the authors' records. The map uses schematic Natural Earth boundaries, not authenticated Survey of India boundaries. Check that depiction before submission. Verify that the cited repository and Zenodo archive expose the final submission version; the archive link could not be independently opened during preparation. The updated local analyses have not been rerun in full as part of packaging.

The editorial-assistance declaration reflects the authors' statement that assistance was limited to manuscript editing, together with preparation of these submission documents. It contains no product names. Both authors approved the signatures and confirmed that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

No publisher copyright or license form has been fabricated. Complete publisher-issued forms when supplied by the journal. If reviewers are requested, enter specialists whose conflicts the authors can assess; no unverified reviewer contact details are supplied.
